Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery Policy

Ethical conduct is the cornerstone of our business. This section outlines our strict zero-tolerance policy against all forms of bribery and corruption, aligning with the principles of the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and all U.S. domestic laws. This policy applies to every employee and agent, ensuring that all business is won and conducted on a fair and honest basis.

Our Strict Policy

Knight Gold Freight strictly prohibits bribery, kickbacks, or other corrupt practices. No employee or agent shall offer, promise, give, solicit, or accept any form of bribe or undue advantage to or from any person, including government officials, clients, or carrier representatives.

Gifts and Hospitality

While we value business relationships, any courtesy such as a gift, meal, or entertainment must be reasonable, modest, and never intended to gain an unfair business advantage. All such activities must comply with our internal monetary limits and require management approval to ensure they are appropriate and transparent.

Carrier Onboarding and Vetting

Safety and reliability are paramount in our operations. Our commitment begins with a rigorous, federally compliant qualification process for every motor carrier we partner with. This section details the mandatory criteria a carrier must meet to be part of our network, ensuring that we only work with safe, legal, and properly insured transportation providers. Interacting with this checklist helps clarify our non-negotiable standards for partnership.

  • ✅
    Active Operating Authority: Carrier must have an active interstate MC Number for the service type.
  • ✅
    Valid USDOT Number: Carrier must maintain a valid and active USDOT registration.
  • ✅
    Current Insurance: Carrier must have required levels of public liability, cargo, and workers' comp insurance, verified via a COI naming us as a certificate holder.
  • ✅
    Satisfactory Safety Rating: We prioritize carriers with "Satisfactory" FMCSA ratings. "Conditional" or "Unsatisfactory" ratings are typically disqualifying.
  • 🚫
    No Coercion: We strictly adhere to anti-coercion rules and will never ask a driver to violate HOS or safety regulations.

Freight Charges and Payment Terms

This section affirms our commitment to transparent and fair financial practices. We operate based on truth-in-billing principles and adhere to all contractual and federal regulations regarding payment. Clarity and timeliness are key to our relationships with both shippers and carriers.

Rate Confirmations

All negotiated rates, accessorial charges, and terms are documented in a legally binding written Rate Confirmation sheet. This is provided to both the shipper and the carrier before any freight is moved to ensure complete alignment.

Timely Payment to Carriers

We are committed to paying our carrier partners promptly, as required by federal regulations (49 CFR § 371.3) and our contracts. Our standard QuickPay option ensures payment within 1-3 business days of receiving proper documentation, unless other terms are agreed upon in writing.

Detention and Accessorials

We honor all agreed-upon terms for detention, layover, and other accessorial charges. We actively track and audit these charges to ensure they are valid and billed accurately to the responsible party.

Recordkeeping and Data Privacy

In our data-driven industry, we are deeply committed to responsible information management. This section covers our dual obligations: first, to maintain transactional records as required by federal law, and second, to protect the confidentiality and security of all personal and business information entrusted to us in compliance with U.S. data privacy laws.

FMCSA Recordkeeping

As a licensed broker, we are required by law (49 CFR Part 371) to retain records of each transaction for a period of three years. This includes names, addresses, rates, and shipment details. These records are maintained in an organized and accessible manner for compliance purposes.

Data Privacy Commitment

We comply with all applicable U.S. data privacy laws. Our collection, use, and disclosure of personal information are governed by our detailed Privacy Policy, available on our website. We implement robust security measures to protect data from unauthorized access or disclosure, ensuring the trust of our clients and partners.
