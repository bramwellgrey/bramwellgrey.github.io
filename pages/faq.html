For Carriers & Drivers

Answers to questions specific to our carrier and driver partners.

Q1: What do I need to start working with Knight Gold Freight as a carrier?

A: To become an approved carrier in our network, you must provide the following:

  • A valid MC Number (Motor Carrier Number) and DOT Number.
  • Proof of current cargo and liability insurance with required minimum coverage and list "Knight Gold Freight" as a certificate holder.
  • A completed W-9 form.
  • A signed Carrier Agreement.
  • References may be requested for new authorities.

Q2: How do you find loads for me?

A: We leverage a combination of our established relationships with shippers, load boards, and our proprietary database to match your truck's location, type, and capacity with available freight. We focus on finding loads that maximize your revenue and minimize deadhead miles.

Q3: How and when will I get paid?

A: We pride ourselves on quick and reliable payment. Our standard payment term is QuickPay within 1-3 business days after you submit the signed Bill of Lading (BOL) and any other required documents. All payments are made via direct deposit or ACH transfer.

Q4: What is your policy on detention, layover, and tonu (truck ordered not used) pay?

A: We advocate fiercely for our drivers' time.

  • Detention: We track your time at shipper/receiver facilities. After the standard 2-hour free time, we pass 100% of the detention fee on to you.
  • Layover: If a load is not ready and causes you to wait for a next-day appointment, we secure layover pay for you.
  • TONU: If you arrive at a pickup location and the load is cancelled, we will charge the shipper a TONU fee, which you will receive in full.

Q5: What happens if there is a claim for damaged or lost freight?

A: As the carrier, you are responsible for the freight from the moment you take possession until it is delivered. We will assist in facilitating the claims process between you and the shipper. We require that you have valid cargo insurance. Immediately notify us of any damage discovered at pickup or delivery.

Q6: Who handles communication with the shipper and receiver?

A: We do. You communicate any issues (delays, breakdowns, problems at the dock) directly to your dedicated dispatcher at Knight Gold Freight. We will handle all communications with the customer, saving you time and stress.

Q7: Do you offer dedicated lanes or consistent freight?

A: Yes. We have several shipper clients with recurring freight needs. Consistent, reliable carriers who perform well are first in line for these dedicated lane opportunities.

For Shippers & Brokers

Answers to questions specific to our shipper and broker clients.

Q8: What is the difference between a broker and a dispatcher? Who are you?

A: Knight Gold Freight operates as a freight broker. We are licensed, bonded, and insured to arrange transportation between you (the shipper) and a motor carrier. We handle the entire process: sourcing, vetting, booking, tracking, and paperwork.

Q9: How do you vet your carriers?

A: Carrier safety is our top priority. Our vetting process is rigorous and includes:

  • Verifying active MC/DOT numbers via the FMCSA SaferSys.
  • Confirming current insurance coverage and requiring certificates directly from the insurer.
  • Reviewing safety ratings and inspection history.
  • Checking for out-of-service violations.

Q10: How are rates determined?

A: Freight rates are dynamic and based on several factors, including market conditions, load details (weight, dimensions), and accessorials (e.g., lumper services). We use industry tools and our market expertise to provide you with a fair and competitive rate.

Q11: What is your process for tracking shipments?

A: We provide proactive tracking. We will notify you immediately of any delays or issues and work on solutions. We use automated ELD integration and direct communication with the driver to monitor pickup, in-transit progress, and delivery.

Q12: What paperwork is required, and how is it handled?

A: We streamline the paperwork process digitally. We prepare the Rate Confirmation sheet and, after delivery, we collect the signed Bill of Lading (BOL) and Proof of Delivery (POD) from the carrier. We process these documents and invoice you electronically.

Q13: What is your claims process for damaged freight?

A: While the carrier is legally liable for cargo damage, we manage the entire claims process on your behalf. You must note any damage on the delivery receipt and notify us within 24 hours of delivery. We will immediately file a claim with the carrier's insurance company and act as your advocate.

Q14: What are your payment terms?

A: Our standard payment terms for shippers are Net 15 or Net 30 from the date of invoice. For new clients, we may require payment before the load is booked until credit is established.
