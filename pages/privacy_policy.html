1. Introduction

Welcome to Bramwell Grey, the online portal for Knight Gold Freight. This section introduces our commitment to protecting your privacy. It explains that by using our website or services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. We encourage you to read this document carefully to understand how we handle your personal data.

Our goal is to be transparent about how we collect, use, and protect your personal information across all our dispatch and freight services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services effectively, we collect certain information. This section breaks down the types of data we gather, which fall into three main categories: information you provide directly, data collected automatically through our website, and information we may receive from third-party partners. The chart below gives a high-level overview of these categories.

A. Information You Provide Directly:

  • Contact Information: Name, company, email, phone number, physical address.
  • Shipment Details: Goods description, value, dimensions, weight, pickup/delivery addresses, and recipient contact info.
  • Business Information: FEIN, DOT/MC numbers, and insurance details for clients or carriers.
  • Correspondence: Records of your emails and other communications with us.

B. Information Collected Automatically:

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to track activity. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser.

C. Information from Third Parties:

  • Carriers and Partners: Shipment status updates.
  • Publicly Available Sources: For business verification.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific business purposes, all aimed at providing you with reliable and efficient service. This includes managing logistics, communicating with you, improving our operations, and ensuring security.

  • To Provide and Manage Services: Facilitating dispatch, coordinating freight, generating quotes, tracking shipments, and handling payments.
  • Communication: Responding to inquiries, sending service updates, and providing customer support.
  • Business Operations: Managing relationships, maintaining records, and improving service quality.
  • Marketing: With your consent, sending newsletters or promotional materials, which you can opt-out of at any time.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: Monitoring for and preventing illegal activities to protect our company and clients.

5. How We Share and Disclose Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it in limited, necessary circumstances to provide our services or comply with legal obligations. This section details who we might share your data with.

  • With Freight Carriers and Partners: Shipment details are shared with drivers and logistics partners to transport and deliver your freight.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third parties for functions like email communication and data analysis. They only have access to the information needed to perform their tasks.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose information if required by law or valid requests from public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred, with prior notice.
  • With Your Consent: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

The security of your data is a top priority. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, it's important to recognize that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy. Our retention periods are determined by our need to comply with legal obligations (such as tax and business record laws), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Once the information is no longer needed for these purposes, it is securely deleted.
