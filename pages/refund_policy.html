2. Nature of Our Services & Fees

This section explains our role as a broker. It's important to understand that our fees are for intellectual and logistical services, which are performed in real-time. This is why our services are generally non-refundable once the work has begun.

  • Brokerage Role: We are a freight broker, facilitating transactions between Shippers and Carriers.
  • Types of Fees: Our fees are a margin on the freight rate or a flat-rate dispatch fee.
  • Non-Refundable Services: Our work begins immediately upon a load being tendered. The time and resources invested in sourcing a Carrier and coordinating logistics are our primary service, which makes them non-refundable.

3. Cancellation Policy by Party

This is a critical section that details the consequences of canceling a shipment. The policy is different depending on who is canceling and when the cancellation occurs relative to the pickup.

A. Cancellation by the Shipper:

  • Before Carrier Assignment: No fee.
  • After Carrier Assignment but Before Pickup: A cancellation fee will be applied to compensate the Carrier and us for lost time and resources.
  • After Carrier Arrival (No-Show): The Shipper will be responsible for 100% of the freight charge, as this is a "truck ordered, not used" (TONU) scenario.

B. Cancellation by the Carrier:

Cancellation by a Carrier is a serious breach of agreement. A Carrier who cancels may be barred from our network and could be held financially liable for the additional costs we incur to find a replacement to serve the Shipper.

4. Refund Policy

This section clarifies when and if a refund may be issued. As our fees are earned upon the successful performance of our brokerage services, they are generally non-refundable.

IMPORTANT - NON-REFUNDABLE FEES:

Brokerage and dispatch fees are generally **NON-REFUNDABLE**. For Shippers, our fee is earned once a Carrier has been assigned. For Carriers, dispatch fees are earned upon the successful matching of a load.

The only exception is if a Shipper is overcharged due to a proven billing error on our part. In that case, we will issue a prompt refund for the overcharged amount.

5. Dispute Resolution for Cancellation Fees & Refunds

This section provides a clear process for you to follow if you wish to dispute a cancellation fee or a refund decision. All disputes must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the invoice date.

We will review all documentation and provide a written response within 14 business days. Our final decision will be based on the terms of this Policy and the specific circumstances of the shipment.
