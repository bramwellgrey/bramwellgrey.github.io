A comprehensive guide to maximizing your income potential through independent contracting, on-demand platforms, and specialized delivery services across the Sunshine State.

Executive Summary

The cargo van delivery market in Florida presents exceptional opportunities for independent contractors, with established courier companies and on-demand platforms creating multiple pathways to consistent income generation.

Primary Opportunity

Independent contracting with courier companies like Blue Streak Couriers, Mojo Courier, and Courier Express offers the highest earning potential with weekly gross incomes between $800-$1,200.

Platform Economy

On-demand services like GoShare ($105/hour potential), Roadie (long-distance gigs), and Frayt ($20-$30/hour average) provide flexible supplementary income streams.

Specialized Niches

Medical courier services, last-mile e-commerce delivery, and legal document transport offer premium rates for drivers with specialized certifications and equipment.

Success in this market requires meeting common requirements including late-model cargo vans (2015+), adequate commercial insurance coverage (100k/300k/50k minimum), and maintaining a clean driving record. While this path offers greater flexibility and higher income potential than traditional employment, it also demands careful management of business expenses, taxes, and administrative responsibilities.
