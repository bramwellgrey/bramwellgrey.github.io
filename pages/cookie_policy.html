2. What Are Cookies?

This section provides a simple explanation of what cookies are and the other similar technologies we might use to track your usage on our site. Understanding these terms will help you better understand the rest of the policy.

  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device that remember your preferences.
  • Similar Technologies: Includes web beacons (for monitoring user behavior), log files (for tracking actions), and local storage (for storing user data on your browser).

We use cookies for four main purposes. These categories help you understand why each type of cookie is necessary and how they improve your experience on our site, from basic functionality to site analytics and advertising.

CATEGORIES OF COOKIES:
